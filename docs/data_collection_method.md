## Information Collected
    - Name/type of product
    - Store name
    - Package size
    - Brand
    - Regular or sale price
    - Collection date
    - Collection source
    - Price

## Collection Rules
Brand is recorded but does not affect ranking, but it can be shown if the user wishes to. Products are compared when they have the same basic criterias (name/type, size, price type). Prices will be collected within the same general time period. Sale and membership prices will be treated differently than regular prices; online and in-store prices will be labeled separately.
